# The Founding Hundred

The Founding Hundred recognizes the first 100 dads who help shape HalfTimeDad before its public launch on October 1, 2026. We are not rewarding people merely for buying first; we are honoring the dads who believed first.

## Offer

- $9.99 monthly or $99 annually
- Founding price locked while membership remains active
- Permanent founder number from #001 to #100
- Build updates, founder voting, resources, and early feature access
- Public recognition is always optional

## Guardrails

- Never imply payment was collected until Stripe confirms the Checkout Session.
- Never display fake founder counts or fabricated profiles.
- Never reveal founder details without explicit permission.
- Always qualify lifetime pricing with “while membership remains active.”
- Assign founder numbers from the database; never duplicate them manually.
