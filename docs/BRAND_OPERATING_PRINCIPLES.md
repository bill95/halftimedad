# HalfTimeDad Brand & Operating Principles

## Mission
No dad should have to navigate life's hardest transitions alone.

## Philosophy
**Perspective before reaction.**

## Voice
Calm, candid, practical, grounded, and welcoming. Use “we” rather than centering a public founder. Avoid combat language, legal promises, macho posturing, and claims that technology replaces human judgment.

## Product test
Build only when it helps a dad make a better decision, reduce stress, strengthen his relationship with his kids, or feel less alone.

## Principles
1. Kids come first.
2. Respond; do not react.
3. Practical beats perfect.
4. Community beats ego.
5. Build with dads.
6. AI supports judgment; it does not replace it.
7. Trust is earned every day.
8. We do not profit from prolonging conflict.

HalfTimeDad provides education and peer-oriented perspective, not legal, medical, mental-health, financial, or emergency advice.
