The Sunday Reset — Issue 2

A completely unofficial way to track the calm.

Somewhere along the way, dads navigating co-parenting start measuring everything in incidents.

How many texts went sideways. How many pickups felt like a trap. How many times something small turned into something that ruined the rest of the day.

What if you tracked the opposite?

12
Days since last incident
Completely unofficial. Not admissible in court.
But worth noticing.

That's the idea behind the Domestic Peace Monitor™ — a private, lighthearted tool for tracking stretches of calm. Not to keep score. Not to build a legal case. Just to notice that things are sometimes okay, and that okay is worth paying attention to.

When you hit a streak, you feel it. And when the counter resets, you're a little more prepared — because you've been here before and you got through it.

How dads are using it

"I started sharing my link with my brother. He texts me when the number goes up. It's become a running joke, but honestly it keeps me accountable."

"I didn't expect to feel proud of a number on a screen. But 30 days is 30 days."

Your monitor is private and lives in your browser — no account required. Share your link with a friend, a sibling, or whoever's in your corner. Let them root for the number.
