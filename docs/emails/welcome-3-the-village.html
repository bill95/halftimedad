The Sunday Reset — Issue 3

One dad answered the phone.

It wasn't a hotline. It wasn't a program. It was just a guy who had been through it — the lawyers, the late nights, the texts that made his hands shake — calling another dad who was right in the middle of it.

He didn't have all the answers. He just had this: I've been where you are. Here's what helped me not make it worse.

That call is where HalfTimeDad started.

Not every dad has someone like that. Someone who's already walked the road and will pick up when things get hard at 9pm on a Tuesday.

That's what we're building — a village that makes that kind of practical perspective available to any dad who needs it, wherever he is.
